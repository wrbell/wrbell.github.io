3M Fulfillment · Dec 2025PDF ready

3M Fulfillment

E-commerce fulfillment network redesign.

HBS-style 3M case — PuLP MILP and distance-tier transport model to expand from 45% to 95.2% 1-day coverage with K=8 DCs (Dallas, Atlanta, LA, Allentown, KC, Denver, Minneapolis, Columbus). Grounded Year 1 operating cost $16.5M (~0.06% of revenue) — a strategic service investment, not a cost-cut.
