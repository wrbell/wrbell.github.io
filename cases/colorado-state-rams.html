§01 · brief

Fan experience as supply chain

One-week sprint on CSU Athletics logistics — mapping concession, retail, and in-stadium flows under game-day demand spikes. The prompt asked us to treat the fan journey as a supply-chain problem: inventory placement, replenishment cadence, and vendor coordination across a 40,000-seat venue.

§02 · model

Network mapping

We mapped inbound vendor lanes, on-site storage, point-of-sale demand curves by quarter, and halftime surge multipliers. The model separated pre-game staging, in-game replenishment, and post-game teardown as distinct operational modes with different labor and inventory constraints.

§03 · recommendation

Replenishment cadence

Our deck reframed fan experience around zone-based micro-replenishment — smaller, more frequent restocks to high-traffic stands instead of centralized pushes at fixed intervals. We paired that with a vendor scorecard tying service level to contract renewals.

§04 · outcome

Live presentation

I led the panel presentation — walking judges through the demand map, our replenishment proposal, and a phased implementation timeline that avoided disrupting the current season’s vendor contracts.
