§01 · brief

Critical materials risk

Two-week case on semiconductor-grade rare-earth supply — mapping extraction, refining, and fab demand under export controls and price volatility. The prompt centered on securing feedstock for advanced packaging and magnet applications when single-country concentration creates policy risk.

§02 · model

Supply map and exposure

We built a tiered supplier map from mine to refiner to OEM, quantifying country concentration, lead times, and substitution difficulty per element. Scenario layers modeled export-ban probability and price spikes on the highest-exposure SKUs.

Supplier map figures — deck pending publication

§03 · recommendation

Dual sourcing and buffers

Recommendation combined dual-sourcing for the three highest-exposure elements, strategic inventory buffers sized to refining lead times, and long-lead contracts with non-traditional suppliers vetted for quality certification timelines.
