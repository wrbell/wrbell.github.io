§01 · brief

Cold chain under margin pressure

24-hour Land O'Lakes case on Kozy Shack pudding distribution — balancing refrigerated lane costs, DC placement, and retailer service levels. Refrigerated freight and spoilage risk dominate the cost structure; the brief required explicit margin guardrails on any network change.

§02 · model

Network and cost model

I owned the network model — lane costs by temperature class, DC-to-retailer assignment, and inventory days-on-hand assumptions. We stress-tested scenarios where consolidated DCs lowered fixed cost but lengthened average transit time in the refrigerated lane.

Model workbook and maps — deck pending publication

§03 · recommendation

Phased cold-chain redesign

The team pitched a phased cold-chain redesign — regional consolidation where lane economics clear a margin hurdle, paired with dedicated reefers on the highest-volume lanes. We flagged SKUs where service level could not be maintained without violating the case’s margin floor.
