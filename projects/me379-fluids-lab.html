§01 · problem path
./problem.md does a delta VG delay stall on a NACA 0015?

boundary_layer_control

Capstone experiment for ME 379 Thermal Fluids Lab: passive boundary-layer control on a NACA 0015 airfoil (175 mm chord, 189 mm span) using delta-shaped vortex generators. Compare baseline vs. VG configurations across angle of attack and tunnel speed — quantify stall delay without a measurable drag penalty near stall.

Team: Willem Bell, Hailei Colvin-Jackson, Aydan Benitezbertheau, Andrey Shturko, Oliver Ullrich, Seth Mckinney. Reference target: Tebbiche & Boutoudj (2015) on delta VGs at Re ≈ 2.5×10⁵.

sting_balance_pipeline

Custom 6061-T6 aluminum sting balance with two HX711 strain-gauge bridges (lift and drag). Arduino Uno samples at 80 Hz to micro-SD; Python pipeline in pipeline/ handles tare → 4th-order Butterworth filter → 2×2 calibration matrix → matched bar subtraction → CL/CD.

§03 · decisions path
./CHANGELOG.md matched bar subtraction · speed interpolation.

tradeoffs_actually_made

  1. sting · subtract

    Matched bar subtraction.

    Bar-only runs at every (orientation, fan Hz) pair subtracted from airfoil runs to remove sting aerodynamic interference — required for defensible absolute CD.

  2. speed · interp

    Interpolated mid-band speeds.

    April 15 added 24/35/40 Hz points between the pitot-calibrated 15/30/45/60 Hz anchors (mph ≈ 1.672·Hz − 4.13, R² > 0.9999). 15 Hz excluded from analysis — insufficient SNR.

  3. vg · tebbiche

    VG placement from literature.

    Delta VG design followed Tebbiche (2015) optimal placement at x/c ≤ 20%. Pre-stall (10°, 12°) baseline and VG curves overlap within error; near-stall 16° shows ΔCL ≈ +0.05 with no drag penalty.

§04 · outcome path
./STATE.md 59 runs · Re 1.1–5.3×10⁵.

key_findings

At Re ≈ 2.8×10⁵: CL(10°) ≈ 0.82, CL(12°) ≈ 1.06, CL(16°) ≈ 1.13 — pre-stall slope within 10% of thin-airfoil 2π/rad, flattening above 12° consistent with separation. Lift magnitudes within ~10% of Tebbiche pre-stall; absolute CD offset ≈ +0.25 attributed to incomplete sting cancellation.

Full repo with data, figures, and CI: github.com/wrbell/ME379.
